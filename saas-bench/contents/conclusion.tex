\section{Conclusion}
\label{con}

We introduced \datasetname, a benchmark for evaluating CUAs in realistic, deployable SaaS environments. Built on 23 real SaaS systems across six professional domains, \datasetname contains 106 tasks that require cross-application coordination, multimodal understanding, and long-horizon execution. Our evaluation shows that current agents still struggle in realistic SaaS workflows, particularly in planning, state tracking, and error recovery. To the question posed in our title---\emph{Can CUAs leverage real-world SaaS to solve professional workflows?}---the answer is: not yet. Even the strongest model completes fewer than 4\% of full workflows end-to-end, and all models exhibit monotonic performance decay as tasks progress, revealing that sustained long-horizon execution and cross-application coordination remain fundamental open challenges. We hope \datasetname can support future research toward more reliable and practically deployable CUAs.
