% In this work, we propose PivotRL, a reinforcement learning algorithm that is able to tractably train LLMs on long horizon agentic tasks through turn-level training. We provide a easy method to identify crucial pivot turns in an existing trajectory, as well as a way to rank such pivots to create a training dataset for standard GRPO-like training. We show experiments across a variety of tasks and benchmarks and show the effectiveness and scalability of our method. In our future work, we plan to expand our setting to incorporate non-programmatic verifiers, such as LLM-as-a-judge \citep{zheng2023judgingllmasajudgemtbenchchatbot} and process reward models \citep{lightman2023letsverifystepstep}, or online reward profiling approaches such as dynamic sampling \citep{yu2025dapoopensourcellmreinforcement}
