\section{Conclusion}
In this work, we present a holistic framework advancing practical GUI agents across three dimensions: data, deployment, and evaluation. 
We introduce the Calibrated Step Reward System (CSRS), a self-evolving pipeline that drastically reduces annotation costs while enabling the training of SOTA Step-GUI models (4B/8B).
To standardize deployment, we propose GUI-MCP, a hierarchical privacy-centric protocol that balances execution efficiency with on-device data security. 
Finally, we contribute AndroidDaily, a benchmark grounded in authentic usage patterns to rigorously evaluate real-world utility.
Together, these innovations bridge the critical gap between research capabilities and reliable daily assistance.
