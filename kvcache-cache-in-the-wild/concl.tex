\section{Conclusion}
\noindent
We present the first systematic and in-depth characterization
of production serving workloads for {\kvcache} cache systems.
Our study reveals several important findings
that were not captured by synthetic workloads used in previous studies.
Based on these findings,
we further propose a workload-aware cache eviction policy
that improves upon existing workload-agnostic policies like LRU and LFU.
We believe our work, as a starting point,
can benefit both the research and industry fields
in improving current and future LLM serving systems with a workload-driven approach.
